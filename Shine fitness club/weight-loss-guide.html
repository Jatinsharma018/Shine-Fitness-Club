3. Fat Loss Ka Science: Calorie Deficit Aur Metabolism

Hamaari body ek engine ki tarah kaam karti hai aur khana uska fuel hai.

A. Calorie Deficit Kya Hota Hai?

Aapki body dinbhar me jitni calories burn karti hai, agar aap usse thodi kam calories khate ho, toh use Calorie Deficit kehte hain. For example: Agar aapki body ko 2000 calories chahiye aur aap 1500 calories khate ho, toh bachi hui 500 calories body aapke stored fat se burn karegi. Isse aapka fat kam hoga.

B. Metabolism Ko Fast Kaise Kare?

Jab aap sudden khana chhod dete ho, toh body 'Starvation Mode' me chali jati hai aur metabolism slow ho jata hai. Sahi protein aur nutrition dekar hi metabolism ko fast kiya ja sakta hai.

5. Bina Bhuke Weight Loss Karne Ke 5 Golden Rules

  1. Har Meal Me Protein Base Rakho: Isse pet lambe time tak bhara rehta hai aur muscles safe rehte hain.
  2. Khane Se Pehle Salad Khaye: Lunch aur Dinner se 15 min pehle ek badi plate salad khaye taaki overeating na ho.
  3. Liquid Calories Band Karo: Cold drinks, packed juice aur zyada meethi chai band karke Nariyal Pani ya Green Tea piyo.
  4. 7-8 Ghante Ki Sahi Neend: Neend poori nahi hogi toh Cortisol (Stress Hormone) badhega aur belly fat kam nahi hoga.
  5. 80% Nutrition + 20% Workout: Weight loss me 80% role aapke khane ka hota hai aur 20% exercise ka.

6. BMI (Body Mass Index) Kya Hai Aur Kyu Zaroori Hai?

BMI se pata chalta hai ki aapki height ke hisab se aapka weight normal hai ya aap overweight category me aate ho.

Formula: BMI = Weight (in KG) / (Height in Meters)^2

  • Below 18.5: Underweight (Kam Weight)
  • 18.5 - 22.9: Normal (Perfect Healthy Weight)
  • 23.0 - 24.9: Overweight (Thoda Zyada Weight)
  • 25.0+ : Obese (Motaapa / Risk Zone)
